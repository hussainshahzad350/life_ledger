# Hydration (Habits & Patterns)

> [Knowledge Base](00-index.md) · Behavior-adjacent. The *substance* is in [water.md](water.md); this
> file is about **hydration behavior** — timing, consistency, and habit design.

## Definition
Hydration is the ongoing *practice* of maintaining adequate body water — a behavior pattern, not a
single number. Distinct from [water.md](water.md) (the nutrient science) because the app's real lever
is helping users build a consistent drinking habit.

## Benefits
- Consistent hydration supports steady energy and concentration through the day. *(B)*
- Spreading intake avoids the late-day "catch-up" pattern that disrupts sleep via nighttime waking. *(C)*

## Deficiency (pattern view)
The typical failure isn't ignorance — it's **forgetting**. Common patterns: under-drinking on busy
days, or logging most water after 6pm. LifeLedger detects the latter with `HYDRATION_TIMING`. *(D at
the individual level — a personal pattern, not a general claim.)*

## Upper Limits
See [water.md](water.md) (hyponatremia); behaviorally, discourage forced over-drinking to "win" a goal.

## Common Misconceptions
- *"I'll just drink a lot in the evening to catch up."* Even total-daily-adequate intake concentrated
  late can disturb sleep. *(C)*
- *"Feeling thirsty is a failure."* Thirst is a normal cue to act on. *(B)*

## Evidence Level
Habit/timing guidance: **C**. Underlying water need: **A/B** (see [water.md](water.md)).
