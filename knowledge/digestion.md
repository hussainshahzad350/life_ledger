# Digestion

> [Knowledge Base](00-index.md) · Health.

## Definition
Digestion is how the body breaks food into absorbable nutrients — a process spanning the mouth,
stomach, and intestines, influenced by food composition, fiber, hydration, stress, and habits.

## Benefits (of good digestive habits)
- Regularity and comfort; better nutrient absorption. *(B)*
- Fiber + water support healthy transit. *(A/B)* → [fiber.md](fiber.md), [water.md](water.md)

## "Deficiency" / Common issues
- Constipation (often low fiber/water/activity), bloating, and irregularity are common and
  **frequently food-related** — the core reason LifeLedger correlates **food ↔ symptoms**. *(B; the
  individual's triggers are D — personal patterns.)* → [symptoms.md](symptoms.md)

## Upper Limits
Rapid large increases in fiber without water can worsen bloating/gas — increase gradually. *(B.)*

## Common Misconceptions
- *"Bloating always means intolerance."* Many causes (meal size, fiber changes, swallowed air). *(C)*
- *"A 'detox' cleanses digestion."* The gut/liver/kidneys already do this; no cleanse required. *(B)*
