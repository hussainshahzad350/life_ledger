# Gut Health

> [Knowledge Base](00-index.md) · Health. An **emerging-evidence** area — tagged and hedged accordingly.

## Definition
"Gut health" broadly refers to the function of the digestive tract and the **gut microbiome** (the
community of microbes living there), which interacts with digestion, immunity, and possibly mood.

## Benefits (of supporting it)
- A diverse, fiber-fed microbiome is associated with better digestion and metabolic markers. *(B/C)*
- Fermentable fibers produce **short-chain fatty acids** beneficial to gut lining. *(C)* → [fiber.md](fiber.md)

## "Deficiency" / Disruptors
- Low-fiber diets, some medications, and low food diversity are associated with less favorable
  microbiome profiles. Individual effects vary widely. *(C.)*

## Upper Limits
No meaningful "too much gut health." Probiotic/prebiotic supplementation effects are variable and
strain-specific — the app makes **no supplement claims**. *(C.)*

## Common Misconceptions
- *"Probiotics fix everything."* Effects are strain- and person-specific; evidence is mixed. *(C)*
- *"Gut health is fully understood."* It's an active research area; LifeLedger avoids over-claiming. *(C)*

## Evidence Level
Mostly **C** (emerging/mechanistic). Fiber's role is stronger (**B**). The app communicates this
uncertainty honestly.
