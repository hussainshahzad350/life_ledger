# Exercise / Physical Activity

> [Knowledge Base](00-index.md) · Health. LifeLedger is **not** a fitness tracker — exercise is
> context, not the product ([docs/01 non-goals](../docs/01-vision.md)).

## Definition
Physical activity is any bodily movement that expends energy; **exercise** is structured, purposeful
activity. Common guidance: **≥150 minutes/week** of moderate aerobic activity plus muscle-strengthening
on ≥2 days.

## Benefits
- Cardiovascular health, insulin sensitivity, and weight management. *(A)*
- **Muscle preservation** (with adequate protein) during weight loss. *(A)* → [protein.md](protein.md)
- Improved mood, sleep quality, and stress resilience. *(B)*

## Deficiency
Physical inactivity is a well-established risk factor for chronic disease. The app encourages movement
supportively, without judgment. *(A.)*

## Upper Limits
Overtraining without recovery can cause fatigue, poor sleep, and injury; recovery (and sleep) matter.
The app doesn't prescribe training loads. *(B.)*

## Common Misconceptions
- *"You can out-exercise a poor diet."* Exercise energy is often smaller than assumed; diet dominates
  weight change. *(A)* → [weight_loss.md](weight_loss.md)
- *"Logged exercise should always be added to my calorie budget."* LifeLedger avoids double-counting:
  activity is already in the TDEE PAL; adding logged exercise is an optional, labeled setting
  ([docs/06 §3](../docs/06-health-rules.md)). *(B)*

## Evidence Level
General health benefits & guidance: **A**. Exercise-energy-in-app accounting: **B** (design choice).
