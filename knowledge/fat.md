# Dietary Fat

> [Knowledge Base](00-index.md) · Nutrition.

## Definition
Fat is an energy-dense macronutrient (**9 kcal/g**, [Atwater](../research/usda.md)) essential for
hormone production, cell membranes, and absorption of fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K). Types include
unsaturated (generally favorable), saturated, and trans (to minimize).

## Benefits
- Concentrated **energy** and essential fatty acids (omega-3/omega-6). *(A)*
- Enables absorption of **fat-soluble vitamins**. *(A)*
- Contributes to satiety and food palatability. *(B)*

## Deficiency
True essential-fatty-acid deficiency is rare on normal diets; very-low-fat intake can impair vitamin
absorption and hormone function. *(B.)*

## Upper Limits
No single UL for total fat, but guidance emphasizes limiting **saturated fat** (commonly < 10% of
energy) and minimizing **trans fat** (WHO calls for elimination of industrial trans fat). AMDR total
fat **20–35% of energy**. *(A.)*

## Common Misconceptions
- *"Eating fat makes you fat."* Total energy balance governs fat gain. *(A)*
- *"All saturated fat is equally harmful."* Nuanced and food-dependent; guidance still favors
  unsaturated sources. *(B/C)*
- *"Dietary cholesterol directly raises blood cholesterol for everyone."* Response is individual. *(B)*

## Evidence Level
Essential roles & AMDR: **A**. Saturated/trans-fat guidance: **A/B**. Individual variation: **C**.
