# Tiny Habits & the Fogg Behavior Model

> [Behavioral Science index](00-index.md). Applied in [docs/16](../../docs/16-behavioral-design.md).

## Concept
**BJ Fogg's Behavior Model: B = MAP** — a Behavior happens when **Motivation**, **Ability**, and a
**Prompt** converge at the same moment. When motivation is low, make the behavior *tiny* (raise
Ability) and anchor it to an existing routine (Prompt). "Tiny Habits" = start absurdly small and let
it grow.

## Why it matters
LifeLedger can't rely on user motivation being high. The winning move is to shrink the behavior until
it's almost impossible to fail: *log one thing*, *tap water once*. Ability beats motivation.

## Evidence
- Fogg Behavior Model / Tiny Habits — BJ Fogg. **B** `[verify]`
- Anchoring new behaviors to established routines. **B/C** `[verify]`

## How LifeLedger applies it
- **Raise Ability:** the < 10s log, recents/favorites, one-tap water, smart defaults ([docs/05 §5.2](../../docs/05-uiux-system.md)).
- **Anchor prompts:** reminders tied to natural moments (after breakfast), not arbitrary times.
- **Tiny first step:** onboarding asks the minimum; logging a single item counts as a win.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Big upfront setup that raises the ability cost before any reward.
- Prompts with no anchor (random pings) that feel like nagging.
