# Streak Psychology

> [Behavioral Science index](00-index.md). Applied in [docs/16](../../docs/16-behavioral-design.md).

## Concept
Streaks leverage **consistency** and **loss aversion**: a growing chain of successful days motivates
continuation and creates a small "don't break the chain" pull. Powerful — and easy to misuse.

## Why it matters
A well-designed streak reinforces the logging habit. A badly-designed one becomes a source of anxiety
and, when broken, a reason to quit ("I lost my 40-day streak, what's the point").

## Evidence
- Loss aversion — Kahneman & Tversky (prospect theory). **B** `[verify]`
- "Don't break the chain" as a consistency device — popular behavioral practice. **C**

## How LifeLedger applies it (the *humane* streak)
- Streaks celebrate consistency (`STREAK_LOG`, [docs/07 §3](../../docs/07-ai-rules.md)) **without punishing**.
- **Forgiveness by design:** a missed day doesn't erase all progress; consider "freeze"/grace so one
  slip isn't catastrophic. Trend and total effort remain visible.
- Streaks are a *bonus*, never the primary metric — understanding is.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Hostage streaks (huge visible counter that resets to zero and shames the user).
- Nagging notifications that weaponize the streak ("Don't lose your streak!!!").
- Making the streak more prominent than the user's actual health data.
