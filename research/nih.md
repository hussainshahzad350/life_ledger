# Source: National Institutes of Health (NIH)

> Part of the [Research Registry](00-index.md). Tier **T1**.

## What it is
The U.S. National Institutes of Health — the primary U.S. biomedical research agency. Of particular
use: the **Office of Dietary Supplements (ODS)** nutrient fact sheets and NIH consumer health
resources.

## Why LifeLedger trusts it
Rigorous, well-referenced, regularly updated nutrient summaries aimed at both professionals and
consumers — ideal for the "Definition / Benefits / Deficiency / Upper Limits" sections of the
knowledge base.
