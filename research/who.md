# Source: World Health Organization (WHO)

> Part of the [Research Registry](00-index.md). Tier **T1** (major guideline body).

## What it is
The World Health Organization is the United Nations agency for international public health. It
publishes population-level guidelines used worldwide.

## Why LifeLedger trusts it
Broad expert consensus, transparent guideline development, and global applicability — a strong fit
for a privacy-first app serving a global audience.
